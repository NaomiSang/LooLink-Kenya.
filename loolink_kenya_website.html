How LooLink works

We help connect your request with a suitable mobile sanitation service provider.

1

Tell us what you need

Send your location, date, type of facility and estimated number of users or guests.

2

We check provider options

We contact service providers to check coverage, availability and the quotation for your requirements.

3

Review the quotation

You review the provider’s price, hire terms, delivery and servicing arrangements before deciding.

4

Confirm directly with the provider

Once you are satisfied, booking, payment and service delivery are confirmed with the provider.
